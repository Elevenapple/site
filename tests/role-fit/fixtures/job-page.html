Staff Product Engineer

Remote — North America  •  Full-time

Northwind builds tools for teams that ship. We're looking for a staff product engineer to own new surfaces end to end, from the first prototype through the version people actually rely on.

You’ll work with design & go-to-market partners, and you will be accountable for what you ship after it launches.

What you’ll do

  • Build the first version of new product surfaces and iterate after launch.
  • Work across the stack in TypeScript and React, with Node services behind them.
  • Design internal tooling that makes the rest of the team faster.
  • Instrument what you ship and use that data to decide what is next.

What we’re looking for

  • Experience taking a product from nothing to production.
  • Strong TypeScript and React fundamentals — 5+ years.
  • Comfort with LLM APIs and agent tooling in production.
  • A track record of owning a system after launch.

Nice to have

  • Developer tools or technical products.
  • Infrastructure familiarity: cloud deployment, Terraform, CI/CD.

We consider qualified applicants regardless of background. If you need an accommodation during the process, tell your recruiter.
